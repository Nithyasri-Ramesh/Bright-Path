## 📖 About BrightPath

BrightPath is an **AI-powered vision assistant** designed specifically for blind and visually impaired individuals. With a simple **tap-anywhere** interface, users can take photos and receive instant, spoken AI descriptions of their surroundings.

### 🎯 The Problem
- **285 million** people worldwide are visually impaired
- Traditional assistive tools are expensive and complex
- Quick, contextual understanding of surroundings is challenging

### 💡 Our Solution
- **Simple interface** - Tap anywhere to take a photo
- **Instant AI analysis** - Powered by Google's Gemini AI
- **Spoken descriptions** - Text-to-speech for immediate understanding
- **No buttons needed** - Just tap, listen, and understand

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎨 User Experience
- **Full-screen tap** - Tap anywhere to capture photos
- **No buttons needed** - Perfect for visually impaired users
- **Audio feedback** - Beep sounds and voice announcements
- **Visual feedback** - Ripple effects and smooth animations
- **Beautiful dark theme** - Easy on the eyes

### 🤖 AI Capabilities
- **Real-time image analysis** - Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash
- **Structured descriptions** - WHAT, DETAILS, PURPOSE, TEXT, SAFETY
- **Spoken responses** - Automatic text-to-speech
- **High accuracy** - Detailed and reliable descriptions

### 📱 Mobile-First Design
- **Responsive** - Works on all screen sizes
- **Touch optimized** - Large tap targets
- **Camera ready** - Uses device camera
- **Landscape support** - Works in any orientation
